Know what this practice is.

The official examination has 45 items and a one-hour limit: 40 scored items plus five unscored trial items. This practice scores all 45 because it cannot identify which official items would be unscored. The questions are original and based on the 2026 California Notary Public Handbook; they are not copied state-exam questions.
